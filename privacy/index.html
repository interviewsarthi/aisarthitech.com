Privacy policy

This policy covers the aisarthitech.com website only.

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by AI Sarthi Tech, Gurugram, Haryana, India (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at contact@aisarthitech.com.

What this website collects

Very little. This website:

  • sets no cookies;
  • uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools;
  • has no forms or accounts;
  • loads its fonts, images and styles from this website itself, not from third parties.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub, and its domain name and email forwarding are run by Cloudflare. Like any web host, they may process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, to deliver the website and keep it secure. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Messages to contact@aisarthitech.com are forwarded by Cloudflare to our Google (Gmail) mailbox. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of our conversation, and we keep them only as long as we need them for that. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Our apps

Apply Sarthi, Prep Sarthi, Live Sarthi and Meeting Sarthi handle data differently from this website. Their own privacy policy is at interviewsarthi.com/privacy.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, such as your emails, by writing to contact@aisarthitech.com. We'll answer within 30 days.

Children

This website is not meant for children, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
