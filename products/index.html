The Interview Sarthi apps. One for every step.

Four apps, each built for one step: finding a job, practising the interview, getting help during it and handling client meetings at work. Every one is free to start.

Step 1 · Find a job

Apply Sarthi

Fully automatic: you don't fill a single form.

A Windows app for your job search. Set it up once with your CV, and Apply Sarthi finds jobs that fit you on different job portals, fills in each application and sends it, every day.

  • Gets jobs from different portals and applies for you
  • Runs by itself, every day
  • Free every week: 20 applications in your first week, then 15 a week

Windows PC, from the Microsoft Store

Prep Sarthi

A real voice interview about your own CV.

No one to practise with? Prep Sarthi's AI interviewer talks to you by voice and asks about your own projects and the job you want, the way a real interviewer would.

  • Follows up when an answer is weak
  • Scores every answer and shows a stronger one
  • Runs in the browser on your phone or computer

Step 3 · The interview

Live Sarthi

Answers from your own resume, during the interview.

Live Sarthi listens to the interviewer's question and shows a suggested answer based on your CV, right alongside your call. Answers start in about a second; timing varies.

  • Based on your own CV and the job you're interviewing for
  • Unlimited interviews during your pass
  • On your Windows PC, or on your phone

Windows 10/11 PC, or your phone
