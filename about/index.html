About us

We build AI that guides people through the hard moments of work.

AI Sarthi Tech is a software business in Gurugram, India. We make the Interview Sarthi apps.

Why “Sarthi”

सारथी (Sarthi) means charioteer: the guide who steers you down a difficult road. That is the job we want our software to do.

Looking for work, getting ready for an interview, answering under pressure and speaking up in a client meeting are some of the most stressful moments of working life. Each of our apps takes on one of them, so you can walk in prepared.

What we make

Our first family of products is Interview Sarthi: Apply Sarthi applies to jobs for you, Prep Sarthi runs voice mock interviews, Live Sarthi helps during the interview and Meeting Sarthi helps in client meetings. They run on Windows PCs, in the browser and on phones.

How we work

Free to start

Every app can be tried for free before you pay anything.

Pay once

Passes run for a fixed time and never renew automatically.

Real replies

Every support email is read and answered by us, usually within a day.

Honest about limits
