Four apps, one for each step.

Use the one you need today. Each app has its own page on interviewsarthi.com with plans, help and downloads.

Step 1 · Find a job

Apply Sarthi

Finds jobs that fit your CV across job portals, fills in each application and sends it. You don't fill a single form.

Windows app · Free every week Visit Apply Sarthi
Step 2 · Practise

Prep Sarthi

A real voice interview about your own CV. An AI interviewer asks about your projects and the job you want, then scores every answer.

Phone or computer · Free 7-minute demo Visit Prep Sarthi
Step 3 · The interview

Live Sarthi

Listens to the interviewer's question and suggests an answer from your own resume, alongside your call.

Windows and phone · 30 minutes free Visit Live Sarthi
Step 4 · At work

Meeting Sarthi

Live captions of what the client says, and a short reply from your own project, ready when you need it.

Windows app · Captions free Visit Meeting Sarthi
