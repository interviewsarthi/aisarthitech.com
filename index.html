FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is AI Sarthi Tech?
AI Sarthi Tech is a software business in Gurugram, India. We build the Interview Sarthi apps: Apply Sarthi, Prep Sarthi, Live Sarthi and Meeting Sarthi.
Where can I get the apps?
Every app has its own page on interviewsarthi.com with plans, help and downloads. The Windows apps come from the Microsoft Store; Prep Sarthi runs in your browser.
Are the apps free?
Every app is free to start: a demo interview, free minutes or free applications every week. Paid passes run for a fixed time and never renew by themselves.

Write to support@interviewsarthi.com or visit the help page. Every message is read and answered, usually within a day.
